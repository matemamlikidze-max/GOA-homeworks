Dexter

Dexter is an acclaimed American crime drama television franchise starring Michael C. Hall as Dexter Morgan, a Miami forensic blood-spatter analyst who leads a secret life as a vigilante serial killer. Based on the novels by Jeff Lindsay, the original series aired on Showtime from 2006 to 2013 and has since expanded into a multi-series universe.
